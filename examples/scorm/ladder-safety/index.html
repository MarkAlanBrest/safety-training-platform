Welcome

This is a minimal example SCORM package for testing the AI instructor workflow. Each page sets cmi.core.lesson_location so you can map narration cues in the script editor.

Inspection

Before climbing, inspect the ladder:

  • Check feet, rungs, and spreaders for damage
  • Look for cracks, bends, or missing parts
  • Confirm locks and labels are legible

Setup

Set the ladder at the correct angle — about one foot out for every four feet up. Secure the top and base before climbing.

Safe climbing

Maintain three points of contact. Face the ladder, avoid overreaching, and never stand on the top cap or top three rungs unless designed for it.
