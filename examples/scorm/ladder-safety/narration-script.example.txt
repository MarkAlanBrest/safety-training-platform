Welcome to ladder safety. This short example course walks through inspection, setup, and safe climbing.

=== intro ===
Welcome to ladder safety. In this lesson we will cover inspection, setup, and safe climbing habits.

=== inspection ===
Before you climb, inspect the feet, rungs, and spreaders. Look for cracks, bends, or missing parts. If anything looks wrong, tag the ladder out of service.

=== setup ===
Set the ladder at the correct angle — about one foot out for every four feet up. Secure the top and base before you step on.

=== climbing ===
Maintain three points of contact, face the ladder, and avoid overreaching. Never stand on the top cap unless the ladder is designed for that use.
